## Datasets for Evidence-Traceability and Literature Synthesis

### 1. **CORD-19 (COVID-19 Open Research Dataset)**
- **Description:** A large collection of COVID-19 research articles, including full texts and metadata, useful for text mining, literature synthesis, and evidence tracing.
- **Link:** [https://www.kaggle.com/allen-institute/CORD-19-research-challenge](https://www.kaggle.com/allen-institute/CORD-19-research-challenge)
- **Use Cases:** Facilitates the development of pipelines for automated literature review, evidence extraction, and traceability metrics in biomedical research.
---
### 2. **LitCovid Dataset**
- **Description:** Curated subset of PubMed articles related to COVID-19, annotated for relevance and categorized by topic, suitable for agent-based literature synthesis.
- **Link:** [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/)
- **Use Cases:** Supporting evidence mapping and developing traceability metrics across biomedical literature.
---
### 3. **SemMedDB (Semantic MEDLINE Database)**
- **Description:** A repository of semantic predications extracted from PubMed/MEDLINE citations, representing entity-relation-entity triples.
- **Link:** [https://skr3.nlm.nih.gov/MedlinePlus/SemMedDB.html](https://skr3.nlm.nih.gov/MedlinePlus/SemMedDB.html)
- **Use Cases:** Extracting structured evidence for literature synthesis and evaluating traceability of evidence to source statements.
---
### 4. **Evidence-Based Medicine (EBM) Data Sets**
- **Description:** Datasets containing structured evidence summaries and clinical guidelines, useful for formalizing evidence traceability.
- **Source:** Various public repositories (e.g., NICE Evidence base, Cochrane Library datasets)
- **Use Cases:** Benchmarking evidence traceability metrics in clinical literature synthesis.
---
### 5. **OpenAlex Dataset**
- **Description:** An open catalog of scholarly works, authors, and venues, providing comprehensive metadata for literature analysis.
- **Link:** [https://openalex.org/](https://openalex.org/)
- **Use Cases:** Building agentic pipelines that require tracking provenance and traceability across scholarly articles.
---
### 6. **Microsoft Academic Graph (MAG)**
- **Description:** Large-scale dataset of scholarly publications, citations, and authorship data.
- **Link:** [https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/microsoft-academic-graph/](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/microsoft-academic-graph/)
- **Use Cases:** Citation-based evidence tracing, synthesis pipeline validation.
